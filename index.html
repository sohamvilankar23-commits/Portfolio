NyayaAI v2

NyayaAI v2 is an AI-powered Indian Judiciary analytics and legal assistance platform designed to help legal professionals analyze cases, understand judicial patterns, identify legal risks, and generate legal documents.

Core Capabilities & Highlights:

  • Precedent Graph Engine: Interactive graphical exploration mapping semantic relationships across Indian court precedents.
  • Legal Risk Detector: Automated heuristic scanner identifying procedural vulnerabilities and contradictory arguments.
  • Procedural Flow & Delay Prediction: Statistical modeling forecasting case milestones and expected delays.
  • Google Gemini AI Legal Chatbot: Domain-grounded conversational assistant for rapid legal queries and statutes.
  • Outcome Calibration & Document Drafting: Predictive outcome probabilities and instant legal notice/petition template drafting.
Tech Stack:
Python FastAPI React.js Vite Machine Learning NLP Google Gemini API MongoDB Datasets (CSV/JSON)

CivicEase

CivicEase is a Flutter-based civic complaint management application that allows citizens to report local infrastructure problems and connect with the appropriate authorities.

Core Capabilities & Highlights:

Full-Stack E-Commerce E-Commerce | Full-Stack Development

UrbanCart

UrbanCart is a full-stack e-commerce platform focused on showcasing and selling handcrafted Indian products. It brings traditional craftsmanship and modern online shopping together. The platform features more than 72 products across six categories, including traditional sarees, handloom clothing, jewellery, handicrafts, home decor, and handmade bags.

Core Capabilities & Highlights:

  • Comprehensive Catalog & Search: 72+ products across 6 artisanal categories with multi-attribute filtering and sorting.
  • Artisan Stories & Reviews: Dedicated product pages highlighting craftsman origins, verified ratings, and customer feedback.
  • Persistent Cart & Auth: User-specific persistent shopping carts, JWT authentication, and secure password hashing.
  • Admin Portal & Analytics: Role-based access control, Cloudinary image uploads, real-time inventory tracking, and administrative analytics.
Tech Stack:
React.js Vite Node.js Express.js Supabase PostgreSQL Cloudinary JWT REST APIs
Deployment Architecture: Vercel (Frontend) Render (Backend)
